Research Interests

My research aims to develop efficient and capable AI systems that can perceive, understand, reason about, and interact with the world. I work at the intersection of machine learning, computer vision, and optimization, with three primary research directions. See more details here.

  • Efficiency optimization: How can we reduce the cost of training, fine-tuning, and inference for modern AI models, including LLMs?
  • Learning frameworks: How can models learn effectively from limited or unlabeled data? Current topics include multimodal learning, agentic reasoning, and generative models for image/3D/video.
  • Network architecture design: How can we design neural architectures that are capable and efficient?
